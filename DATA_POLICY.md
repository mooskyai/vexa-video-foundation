# Data Policy

## Principle

A from-scratch model is still only as defensible as its training data. 'No third-party model' does not mean 'any data is acceptable'.

## Allowed data classes

Training assets must fall into an approved class:

- generated by this project's deterministic synthetic renderers;
- created/owned by the project/team;
- explicitly licensed for the intended training use;
- public-domain material with verified status;
- opt-in contributor material under documented terms.

## Required manifest fields

Every real asset record should eventually include:

```text
asset_id
source_uri_or_internal_reference
source_type
license_or_permission
acquired_at
content_hash
creator/provider when known
allowed_uses
removal_status
split
caption/script provenance
processing lineage
```

Do not put secrets, access tokens, or private filesystem paths into release manifests.

## Forbidden ingestion behavior

- no undocumented web scraping into the training set;
- no 'download first, decide later' corpus building;
- no mixing unknown-license assets into approved shards;
- no private user media reuse for model training without explicit permission;
- no synthetic captions from external AI APIs in the default from-scratch lineage.

## Removal

Dataset tooling must support removal by `asset_id`, source/provider, hash, or manifest query, followed by shard regeneration. Training runs must record exactly which manifest version they used.
